Build Your First Neo4j Graph

How this is marked
What to build
Create a graph representing a learning platform.

Create nodes such as

Student

Course

Mentor

Skill

Project

Company

Create meaningful relationships such as

(Student)-[:ENROLLED_IN]->(Course)

(Mentor)-[:TEACHES]->(Course)

(Course)-[:TEACHES_SKILL]->(Skill)

(Student)-[:BUILT]->(Project)

(Project)-[:USES]->(Skill)

(Student)-[:INTERESTED_IN]->(Company)

Create at least 20 nodes and 25 relationships.

Write Cypher queries to create, read, update, and delete data and demonstrate the resulting graph.
